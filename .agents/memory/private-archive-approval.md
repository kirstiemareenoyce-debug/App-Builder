---
name: Private archive approval
description: Scope of the user's approval for enabling Support Archive storage.
---

The user approved private Support Archive saving, with owner-only read, edit,
and delete access. This approval does not enable partner sharing, partner
connections, assessments, or sending their writing to AI.

**Why:** The user selected “Enable private saving” after being explicitly told
the change would preserve existing data and would not enable sharing or AI use.

**How to apply:** Treat private storage and partner/AI processing as separate
permissions. Obtain fresh authorization before activating those other services;
the presence of sharing controls in the imported app is not authorization.
